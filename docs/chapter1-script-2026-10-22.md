## 1. Election morning at home

The television announces registration today and voting tonight. A creditor knocks. Mitko wants the mayor's salary, warm office and official telephone. There is no real-time deadline.

Mitko takes his accordion. His photograph is locked inside the tall wooden cupboard beside the apartment door. Unpaid bills remain optional comic belongings, not a qualification or diploma ingredient.

## 2. First puzzle: the photograph cupboard

Use or Take on the cupboard opens a genuine 3D sliding-block lock built in Blender, based on the owner's wooden puzzle photograph. The 4 by 5 tray contains one large blue block, five yellow rectangles and four small red blocks. Two grid spaces are empty.

Goal: move the blue block down to the central bottom opening. Pieces slide one square orthogonally into free space; they cannot overlap, rotate or leave the tray. This beginner layout takes six moves: upper-left yellow up, lower-left yellow up, lower-right yellow up, bottom-left red left, bottom-right red right, blue down.

Click a block then a direction, or use arrow keys. A hint names the next block and direction without solving it automatically. Reset returns to the easy initial arrangement. Closing and reloading preserve moves. Solving grants one photograph and opens the cupboard permanently. The original photograph remains reusable and recoverable if dropped.

Mitko: "There I am! A face fit for a poster. And, with a little assistance, higher education."

## 3. Lelya Pepa's favor and the campaign poster

Pepa likes Mitko very much and flirts openly. Her help is personal, not a paid paper exchange. She wants him to play a little accordion music for her.

Pepa: "Well, hello, handsome! A campaign poster? Play me a little accordion first. Then bring your photograph and I'll make you look positively presidential."

Once Mitko plays for her, the favor stays fulfilled. She scans his photograph and prints a campaign poster, returning the original photo. No Tony diploma is required for the poster. Mitko can post it on the existing noticeboard, revealing the journalist and opening the established public-campaign route into the municipality.

The poster and altered diploma are separate items. The same photograph serves both; no second photo, second puzzle or repeated mandatory performance is required. Music and fountain work may be done in either order.

## 4. Fountain repair and Baba's support

Baba asks Mitko to fix something, not promise five years. The valve is stuck. Kiro supplies 200 ml sunflower oil in a rakia bottle. Mitko uses the oil on the valve and operates the fountain. The retained approved repair sequence and visible water stream remain unchanged.

Baba supports him after the repair. The oil bottle becomes an empty rakia bottle, which Mitko fills at the running fountain with its murky olive-brown water. Existing bottle inspection, liquid level and close-up controls remain unchanged. The water stays in this bottle for the contest, not a recognizably sober water glass.

## 6. Pepa's Photoshop education

Bring Tony's diploma and Mitko's photograph to Pepa. Her accordion favor must already be fulfilled. She substitutes Mitko's photograph on Tony's diploma in her Photoshop and returns a diploma bearing Mitko's face. The original Tony diploma is consumed in this transformation; Mitko keeps his photograph.

Pepa: "Tony's diploma, your photograph... a little Photoshop, and look! An education with your face on it. I've returned your photograph too."
